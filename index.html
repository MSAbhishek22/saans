    How this works

    1. Real data, live. Every number on this page is fetched at the moment you open it from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API, which serves the Copernicus CAMS forecast. There is no stored or made-up AQI anywhere in this app.
    2. Simple rules, not a black box. The planner looks at the forecast hour by hour and picks the cleanest two-hour windows left today. The advice comes from a short rule table, written by hand:
    When outdoor exercise is fine, by profile
    ProfileGo out if AQI isWhy
    General150 or underHealthy adults cope up to Unhealthy
    Child100 or underChildren breathe faster and are still growing
    Elderly100 or underOlder lungs and hearts feel it sooner
    Asthma / heart50 or underOnly Good air is safe to push in

    Built during EvoHacks 2, 21–22 Nov 2026. The whole app was written in the event window. AI helped draft code and wording, but a person wrote the rules, picked the data source, tested every screen, and can explain each line. No pre-existing code was reused.

    Forecast data comes from a weather model. For medical decisions, follow official CPCB readings and your doctor.
